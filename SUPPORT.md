# Support

## Before reporting a problem

1. Confirm that macOS is version 14 or later and that the AirPods are connected.
2. Move HeadUp to the **Applications** folder before enabling launch at login.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness** and allow HeadUp.
4. In HeadUp, choose **Settings → Test Reminder** to verify the reminder path.
5. Quit and reopen HeadUp, then repeat the two-stage calibration.

## Report an issue

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/breezedeus/head-up/issues) and include:

- macOS version and Mac model.
- AirPods model and firmware version.
- HeadUp version shown in Settings.
- The text from **Settings → Copy Diagnostic Info**.
- What you expected and what happened.

Do not attach raw motion data or other private information. HeadUp has no account system or in-app diagnostic upload.

## Scope

HeadUp is a wellness utility, not a medical device. It estimates head posture from compatible AirPods and cannot diagnose full-body posture or health conditions.
